Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. This policy covers the BlueFind app for Android (package uk.jj.bluefind) and this website. BlueFind is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Everything BlueFind keeps stays on your phone, encrypted. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to anyone else.
  • BlueFind goes online only to download OpenStreetMap map pictures, and that can be switched off.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The live list of devices around you is kept in memory only and is gone when the app closes.

What stays on your phone

BlueFind keeps these in the app's private storage, encrypted with a key held by your phone's secure hardware:

  • The devices you name, tag, favourite, hide, calibrate or watch, with your notes.
  • For watched devices, the times and places they were with you, for 30 days.
  • For tags that are not yours, where they were seen, for 2 days only, to spot one that follows you.
  • Your safe places, saved filters and settings.

Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for BlueFind.

Your location

BlueFind reads your location from your phone's own location service only when you allow it, only while the background watch is on, and only when a watched device or an unknown tag is around or an alert is due. It does not use Google Play services and never sends your location anywhere.

Map pictures

When you open a map, BlueFind downloads the map pictures for the area on screen from OpenStreetMap's tile server. Like any web request this shows OpenStreetMap your IP address and the area being viewed, but nothing about you or your devices. Pictures are kept on the phone for 30 days. Switch Map pictures off in Settings to keep BlueFind fully offline.

Backup files

A backup is made only when you ask for one. The file is locked with a password you choose (PBKDF2 and AES-256) and saved where you pick. BlueFind never sends it anywhere, and without the password nobody can open it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Settings, Delete all BlueFind data removes everything at once. Each device's history can be cleared on its own, and uninstalling BlueFind deletes everything it has kept.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands on your phone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. The only script on it unlocks the download once the lawful-use box is ticked. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 23 November 2026.
