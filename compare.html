What BlueFind does not do

BlueFind cannot find a device that is switched off or out of Bluetooth range, and it does not use any maker's finding network, so it cannot show where a tag is when you are far from it. It is not on Google Play and does not update itself.

Where the others do more

UFind and Wunderfind are on Google Play, so they install and update from there. UFind is available in more languages.
