Release notes

What changed in each version of BlueFind. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.2 23 November 2026Latest

  • The list and radar switch at the top now appears only once there are devices to show.

Version 1.0.1 23 November 2026

  • Fix: 1.0.0 closed as soon as it was opened. BlueFind now opens normally. Install 1.0.1 over 1.0.0.

Version 1.0.0 23 November 2026

  • First release. Find lost Bluetooth devices by their signal.
  • Scanner for low-energy and older Bluetooth together, with makers from the Bluetooth and IEEE registers, smoothed signals and distances, and devices that change their address kept as one.
  • Finding mode with a hotter-colder glow, clicks and vibration, turn to find, calibration at one metre and play a sound on tags that support it.
  • Names, colours, tags, notes, favourites and hidden devices, search, filters and saved filters, list and radar views.
  • Watched devices with last-seen places on an OpenStreetMap map, 30 days of history, left-behind alerts and safe places.
  • The tag check: a warning about any tag that is not yours and seems to travel with you, with plain advice.
  • Home-screen widget, quick settings tile, app lock with PIN or fingerprint, and backup to a file locked with your password.
  • Dark and light themes, larger text, reduced motion and screen-reader descriptions.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.2 is called BlueFind.apk. It is 2.6 MB (2,619,384 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

552e36e5e28e08a788e19ff8ffcaada72bd8b098f4af3a58669fe018488825da

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile BlueFind.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
