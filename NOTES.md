BlueFind 1.0.0, first release.

- Finds lost Bluetooth earbuds, headphones, tags, watches, speakers and car kits by their signal, with the maker and a distance for each.
- Finding mode with a hotter-colder glow, clicks and vibration, turn to find, calibration at one metre, and play a sound on tags that support it.
- Names, colours, tags, notes, favourites, search, filters, list and radar views.
- Watched devices: last-seen places on a map, 30 days of history, left-behind alerts and safe places.
- The tag check warns about any tag that is not yours and seems to travel with you.
- Home-screen widget, quick settings tile, app lock with PIN or fingerprint, and backup to a file locked with your own password.

Android 8 or later. Use it to find your own things and to check that nobody is tracking you, never to follow or locate another person. Check the file against SHA256SUMS before installing.
